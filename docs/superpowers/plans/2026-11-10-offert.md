# Produits offerts — plan de construction

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (mode natif choisi : « en économie »,
> une seule relecture à la fin). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** an associé gives away products of his rayon: stock goes down, no money moves, each gift is valued at the cost
price copied at that moment and listed per associé in Caisse › « Offerts ».

**Architecture:** migration V28 (cost price on products, `gifts` table, two stock-movement kinds); a `giftMethods`
mixin in `worker/gifts.js` like `worker/credit.js`; three routes in `worker/api.js`; an « Offrir » dialog on the
product sheet and list (`src/pages/Products.jsx`); a « Offerts » tab (`src/components/cash/GiftsTab.jsx`).

**Tech Stack:** Cloudflare Worker + Durable Object SQLite, React 18, node:test with `test/harness.js`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-offert-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Amounts are integer hundredths, like `products.price` (`UM` in tests).
- Only an associé gives, and only in his rayon: `user.role === 'associe' && ownsAccount(user, accountOfCategory(p.category))`.
- The gérant never receives `cost_price` (products list, product sheet, demo space).
- A gift never writes `payments`, `charges`, `ledger_entries` or moves a caisse balance.
- Gifts are dated `bizToday()`; `assertOpen(bizToday(), …)` guards gift and cancellation.
- French UI, vouvoiement, no jargon; no real names in code or tests.

## Review Focus

- Two quick clicks on « Offrir » → one gift only (dialog keeps one idempotency key, like the stock dialog).
- Stock changed by someone else while the dialog is open → 409 `STOCK_INSUFFISANT`, nothing written (Task 2 test).
- Cost price edited after a gift → the gift keeps its copied value (Task 2 test).
- An associé whose account is deactivated or moved to the other rayon → only the administrator cancels his gifts (Task 2 test).
- Gérant calling the gift routes directly → 403, and `/api/products` has no `cost_price` key for him (Tasks 1–2 tests).

### Task 2: Giving, cancelling, listing (server)

- `offerProduct(actor, productId, { quantity, member_id?, reason? }) → { gift, product }`.
- `cancelGift(actor, giftId, { reason }) → { gift, product }`.
- `listGifts(actor, { from, to }) → { from, to, by_associate: [{ user_id, name, account, total_cost, quantity,
  missing_cost }], gifts: [giftDTO] }` — associé sees his account only; totals skip cancelled gifts; `unit_cost` null
  counts 0 and raises `missing_cost`.
- Routes: `POST /api/products/:id/offer` (`{}` options; role checked inside), `POST /api/gifts/:id/cancel` (CLUB),
  `GET /api/gifts?from&to` (CLUB).
